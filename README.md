# Mountain Briefing — live feed

The published safety-briefing feed for the Mountain Briefing iOS app (Table Mountain ·
Lion's Head · Signal Hill · Newlands Forest).

- `feed.json` — areas, trails, parking notes and **publicly-sourced incident reports**
  (every incident carries its source name + URL). Curated and human-reviewed before
  every publish; nothing is auto-published.
- Consumed by the app via the raw URL; the app also ships a bundled copy and works
  fully offline after one sync.

**Not a safety guarantee.** This is a digest of public reports, weather-adjacent advice
and daylight information — the hiker decides. Absence of data means absence of *data*,
never safety.
