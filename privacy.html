Mountain Briefing — Privacy Policy

Effective 9 July 2026

Mountain Briefing shows recent safety reports, weather, daylight and hiking routes for Table Mountain, Lion's Head, Signal Hill and Newlands Forest. It is built to collect as little as possible — there is nothing to sign up for and nothing about you is stored anywhere.

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Your location stays on your phone

If you allow location access, it is used only to show your position on the trail map, on your screen. Your location is never transmitted, logged or stored — it never leaves your phone.

What the app fetches

The app downloads two things over the internet: a safety-report feed (hosted on GitHub) and weather forecasts (Apple Weather, with met.no as fallback). Weather is requested for the four fixed public hiking areas — never for your own position. These servers see the same standard technical metadata as any web request (such as an IP address); none of it is available to us or linked to you.

Beta testing

If you use the app through TestFlight, Apple provides us basic beta information (such as crash reports and any feedback you choose to send) under Apple's privacy terms.
