Mountain Briefing — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · replaces the 9 July 2026 version

Mountain Briefing helps visitors hike Table Mountain, Lion's Head, Signal Hill and Newlands Forest: routes, recent safety reports with their sources, park notices, weather and daylight. It is built to collect as little as possible — there is nothing to sign up for, and nothing about you is stored anywhere but on your own phone.

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Your location stays on your phone

If you allow location access, it is used for two things, both on your phone only:

  • Showing where you are on the trail map.
  • Recording a hike, if you start one. While a recording is running the app keeps receiving your position with the screen off (iOS shows its location indicator the whole time), so the track you walked can be drawn on the map. The track is saved only on your phone, and recording stops when you end it.

Your location is never transmitted, uploaded or shared — it never leaves your phone.

What the app fetches

The app downloads three things over the internet:

  • the routes, safety-report and park-notice feed (hosted on GitHub);
  • weather forecasts (Apple Weather, with MET Norway as a fallback) — requested for the four fixed public hiking areas, never for your own position;
  • the Table Mountain Aerial Cableway's published operating status.

These servers see the same standard technical information as any web request (such as an IP address); none of it is available to us or linked to you. The map itself is built into the app and downloads nothing.

When you leave the app

Some buttons hand you over to another app or website, which then has its own privacy policy: Get there opens Uber, Apple Maps or Google Maps with the route's drop-off point (a public trailhead — not your position); emergency numbers open your phone's dialer; and source links open the original article or SANParks page in your browser.

The widget

The home-screen widget shows a summary the app saves on your phone. It is shared between the app and the widget on the device only.

Crash reports and beta testing

If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. If you use a test version through TestFlight, Apple also provides basic beta information (such as crash reports and any feedback you choose to send). Both are under Apple's privacy terms.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
