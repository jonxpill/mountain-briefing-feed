Mountain Briefing — Support

Hiking Table Mountain, briefed.

In an emergency

Don't use email. On the mountain, call Wilderness Search and Rescue: 021 937 0300, or 112 from any phone (free, works without a local SIM). The app's Emergency numbers button has these one tap away.

Questions and feedback

Email jonxpillemer@gmail.com — bug reports, a route you'd like added, or a report or notice you think is wrong (please include the link to your source).

Common questions

Does it work without signal? Yes, once you've opened it on Wi-Fi or data. Routes, the map, the latest reports and your last weather update are kept on your phone.

Where do the safety reports come from? Published news reports, hiking safety sites and SANParks. Every report shows its source and how long ago it happened, and older reports fade out. The app never calls anywhere "safe" — you decide.

Where do park notices come from? SANParks' own notices (closures for weather, fire or trail works). Closures can go up at short notice, so the app always links to SANParks' notices page too.

Does it track me? No. Your location never leaves your phone — see the privacy policy.
